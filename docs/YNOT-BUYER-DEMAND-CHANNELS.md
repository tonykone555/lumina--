# YNOT buyer acquisition channels — 2026-10-13

## Saved plan

Two complementary tracks:

1. **Direct demand**: Research recent explicit furnishing requests from public wanted listings (Craigslist, Gumtree where allowed), BiggerPockets and vacation-rental host communities. Record exact request, source URL, date, budget/location, platform-permitted response method, and product match. Do not automatically message users or harvest private contact details. Pilot: 50 recent verified requests.
2. **Move-in distribution**: Research 30 actively leasing/new apartment developments in UK and US. Find public professional leasing office/business contacts; offer opt-in YNOT Room QR move-in shopping guide and tracked referral/commission link. Pilot: 30 developments.

## Matching

Use existing YNOT Shopify catalogue and Grok MCP product tools. Prioritize seats, sofas, wood/rattan/bamboo furniture, mirrors, lamps, tables, storage, kitchen, pet furniture; verify live prices, images, stock, destination shipping and margins. Use YNOT retail links; keep original manufacturer/provenance information for compliance.

## Prioritization

Explicit recent purchase needs > actively opening/renovating property > general area/move-in indicator. Score evidence, timeliness, channel accessibility, product compatibility, profit and shipping. Mark guesses as research leads, not verified buyers.

## Separate home-purchase leads

Zillow Premier Agent and Realtor.com Connections Plus collect live buyer inquiries but license access primarily to real-estate agents/brokerages, not general furniture retailers; do not assume YNOT can buy/reuse those people's contact information. Zillow/Rightmove/Apify property listings primarily return sales/agent data, not opt-in homebuyer contacts. Seek furniture-specific opt-in leads, builder/agent introduction partnerships, and buyer-facing YNOT Room offers. No homeowner skip-tracing or unsolicited personal-contact enrichment.

## Control

Do not publish, send outreach, or incur paid data costs without approval. Track provenance and permissions, and do not rely on restricted Supabase Growth CRM until migrated.
