# Lumina @ adaptive-world redesign

This build uses `Lumina_At_Stage3_Fixed.zip` as its master source and preserves the Stage 3 product-world interactions.

## New visual system

- premium realistic environment scenes
- translucent left-oriented navigation on desktop and mobile
- conversational search and five niche shortcuts
- refractive product bubbles and pale semantic directions
- clean right-side retail detail panel
- minimal off-white, charcoal and contextual green palette

## Adaptive environments

Natural-language searches are classified into Fashion, Fitness, Hair, Skin, Smile, Home, Tech, Coffee, Outdoor, Kitchen, Pet, Travel, Kids or a neutral premium Retail world. Shopify catalog results remain live; the background, semantic label and refinement controls change without replacing the interface.

## Preserved Stage 3 behavior

Pan and zoom, semantic branches, live Shopify Catalog search, draggable product tags, negative preferences, product-level @ refinement, product pinning and blending, breadcrumbs, taste memory, discovery pockets, Drop Anything and retailer links.
